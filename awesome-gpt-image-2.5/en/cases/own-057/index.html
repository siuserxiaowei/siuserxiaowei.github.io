Complete submitted prompt

The prompt is kept in its original language to preserve exact wording and Chinese typography. The English text above is an editorial guide.

Create a vertical cinematic portrait photo of a young East Asian woman in a romantic ancient Chinese hanfu style, sitting barefoot on a stone railing beside a lotus pond at sunset. She wears a flowing translucent pale pink silk hanfu with layered gauze sleeves, delicate floral embroidery, a soft peach-pink inner bodice, and long draping fabric pooling around her legs. Her black hair is styled loosely up with wispy bangs and small pink-and-white floral hair ornaments and ribbons. She smiles gently at the camera with a soft, dreamy expression while holding one large pink lotus flower on a long green stem in both hands. The setting is a classical Chinese garden pavilion corridor: dark wooden doorway and pillar on the right, gray stone wall and railing on the left, a pond filled with green lotus leaves and scattered blossoms behind her, distant pavilion silhouette and trees across the water. Warm golden sunset light glows low on the horizon, reflecting across the pond and backlighting the sheer fabric, creating a hazy nostalgic atmosphere. Include vertical Chinese calligraphy on the right wooden pillar reading 荷风送香气. Use an analog film look with visible grain, slight softness, warm faded colors, subtle lens flare, gentle overexposure near the sun, and a small orange film date stamp near the lower left edge reading 05 21 '99. Composition should be full-body seated, centered slightly right, portrait orientation 9:16, intimate and elegant, with no modern objects, no extra people, no watermark, and no distorted hands or feet.
