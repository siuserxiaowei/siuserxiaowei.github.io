Create a vertical 9:16 cinematic 3D animated-style scene in a warm sunlit kitchen about dirty produce and invisible germs. In the foreground on a rustic wooden countertop, show exactly 5 anthropomorphic vegetables/fruits with worried cartoon faces: 1 large red apple in the center with big eyes, raised brown eyebrows, open anxious mouth, water droplets, dirt specks, a stem and one green leaf; 1 red tomato at front left with big eyes and shocked open mouth; 1 pale green lettuce/cabbage head at back left with large eyes, frightened mouth, dirt splashes, and leafy folds; 1 tall orange carrot at back right with leafy green top, dirt on its skin, big eyes and sad frown; 1 small broccoli head partly hidden at far right with worried eyes. Add exactly 7 visible small pests on the produce: 1 cute brown snail with eye stalks sitting on the lettuce, 1 green caterpillar crawling on the apple, 3 black flies with translucent wings perched on the apple/tomato/leaf area, and 2 tiny worm-like bugs near the tomato edge. Scatter clumps of dark soil and crumbs across the table and on the produce. At bottom right foreground, place a large handheld magnifying glass angled over the apple, revealing enlarged colorful cartoon microbes inside the lens, including round spiky germs and rod-shaped bacteria in green, yellow, orange, and red, sharply detailed compared with the rest of the scene. Background: cozy kitchen with shallow depth of field, potted plants on a sunny windowsill, sink and jars softly blurred. Include two framed signs: on the left wall a cream poster with hand-lettered Spanish text FRUTAS Y VERDURAS MÁS LIMPIAS = UNA FAMILIA MÁS SANA and a small red heart below; on the right a black chalkboard with white chalk Spanish text LO QUE NO VES TAMBIÉN IMPORTA. Visual style: high-quality Pixar-like clay/stop-motion 3D, expressive oversized eyes, tactile vegetable textures, realistic dirt and water droplets, macro photography feeling, warm golden morning light, soft bokeh, crisp foreground focus, wholesome public-health advertisement mood. Use dirty fruits and vegetables as the scene theme, with warm golden kitchen sunlight and concerned but charming and educational. Avoid logos, watermarks, extra text, extra produce characters, or additional pests beyond the counted visible items.
