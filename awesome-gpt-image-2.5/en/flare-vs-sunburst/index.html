Keep the comparison reproducible

  • Record the actual model identifier, request quality and size, prompt and reference files.
  • Measure first-success time, retries and total time separately.
  • Track exact text, product identity, layout completion and edit drift as separate outcomes.
  • State the sample size and review method; avoid a stable winner claim from a single pair.
  • Keep failures and revisions beside the final output.
